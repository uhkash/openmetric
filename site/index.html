Blindspots: what your usage data is wrong about

A dashboard tells you what you spent. OpenMetric also tells you what the number is missing — with evidence and the command that fixes it.

Unpriced spend

Calls shown as $0 because no rate is known. Your real total is higher than the dashboard says — so it says so.

Untagged traffic

Spend that arrived with no project. Real money, unassignable, flagged.

Error burn

Money spent on failed calls. Input tokens are usually still billed; retries multiply it.

Idle & shared keys

Keys unused for 30 days are a leak risk. Keys shared across projects mean an invoice you can never split.

Budgets & trends

Month-to-date against a limit you set; this period against the last one.

Concentration & latency tails

One provider carrying 90% of spend. A p95 three times the mean.
